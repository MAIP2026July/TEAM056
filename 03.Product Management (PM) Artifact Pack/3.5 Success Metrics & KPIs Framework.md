**3.5 Success Metrics & KPIs Framework**

To connect technical AI behavior to hospital business outcomes and safety guardrails, we track three linked metric tiers:

|Metric Category|Specific KPI / Metric|Baseline (As-Is Est.)|Pilot Target (12 Wks)|Measurement & Verification Method|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Product / Operational|Average Discharge Chart Completion & Coding TAT|48–72 hours post-discharge|< 18 hours (>=60% reduction)|Timestamp delta from physical discharge to MRD 'Chart Ready' lock|
|Product / Operational|Active Coder Review Time per Complex Surgical Chart|18–30 minutes|7–10 minutes|UI session telemetry from chart open to code confirmation|
|Business Value|Initial TPA / Insurance Claim Denial Rate (Coding/Doc Causes)|12%–16% of TPA claims|< 5% (vs. non-pilot control)|Monthly TPA rejection reason-code reconciliation report|
|Business Value|Mock NABH Audit Documentation Deficiency Rate|15%–20% of sampled files|< 4% of pilot files|Monthly blind audit of 50 random charts by MRD Dept Head|
|Trust & Guardrail|Calibrated Coder Acceptance Rate (Accept without Edit)|N/A (100% manual)|72%–85% (Healthy zone)|System telemetry cross-checked against 10% senior manual audit|
|Trust & Guardrail|Rubber-Stamp / Automation Complacency Rate|N/A|< 2% discrepancy on seeded audits|Discrepancy between Coder Accepted AI codes and blind Senior Audit|
