**3.1 Problem Statement & Product Vision**

**Formal Problem Statement:** Hospital Medical Records Department (MRD) Coding Officers and Deficiency Officers in mid-to-large Indian hospitals spend 15 to 30+ minutes per complex inpatient chart manually flipping through post-discharge summaries and operative notes to verify NABH documentation completeness and assign WHO ICD-10 codes. Because physician documentation varies widely in terminology, shorthand, and structure—and because all charts enter an unprioritized FIFO queue—the department experiences 48–72+ hour chart closure turnarounds, inconsistent coding across officers, preventable TPA/insurance claim rejections (MediCodio AI), and last-minute compliance backlogs ahead of NABH surveillance audits.
Product Vision: Empower hospital MRD teams with an explainable, privacy-preserving AI review copilot that pre-screens 100% of discharge summaries for mandatory documentation completeness, recommends evidence-linked ICD-10 codes with calibrated confidence, and triages charts by financial and audit risk—cutting chart closure TAT by over 50% while preserving 100% human clinical and coding authority.
