**3.6 How PM Artifacts Change When the Product Is AI-Powered (Section 8 Requirement)**

A core learning objective of MBA ZG583 is demonstrating how traditional deterministic software artifacts must evolve when managing probabilistic AI systems. Below is our explicit mapping across all eight required PM artifacts, together with how AI tools assisted our PM process versus which critical judgments remained strictly human:

|Standard PM Artifact|What Changes in an AI Product (General Principle)|How This Changed Specifically in Chart Ready AI|
|---|---|---|
|1. Problem Statement|Must define the specific repeated decision, prediction, or judgment workflow being improved rather than missing UI buttons.|Framed around semantic clinical sufficiency checking and variable-text ICD-10 mapping where deterministic regex/checkboxes fail.|
|2. Persona & User Story|Must explicitly account for model uncertainty, confidence calibration, trust, and asymmetric error costs.|Added confidence badges, mandatory verbatim span highlights, and disabled one-click approval on <65% confidence suggestions.|
|3. PRD|Must specify data quality assumptions, out-of-distribution (OOD) fallback logic, and hallucination guardrails.|Defined OCR legibility floors (<75% routes to manual), top-50 code boundary, and zero auto-posting to hospital billing.|
|4. Roadmap|Must replace fixed feature delivery dates with staged data/model learning loops and quantitative proof gates.|Structured into Shadow Validation -> Single-Dept Pilot -> Multi-Specialty Scale, gated by recall, TAT, and audit accuracy thresholds.|
|5. Metrics|Must link technical model metrics (precision/recall/calibration) to business outcomes and human-override guardrails.|Paired Top-3 ICD-10 Recall and ECE calibration with TPA denial reduction and an upper-bound complacency alert (>95% acceptance).|
|6. Launch Checklist|Must include human review workflows, instant kill-switch/rollback mechanisms, and clinical escalation paths.|Added one-click revert to manual FIFO queue if LLM API latency >15s or weekly audit error rate exceeds 5%.|
|7. Pricing / Economics Note|Must account for variable per-transaction token inference, OCR, vector storage, and human review labor costs.|Modeled per-chart token cost (~₹4.20/chart) plus reduced HITL review time against ₹235/chart gross labor and denial savings.|
|8. Review Memo|Must separate generative AI hype ('100% autonomous coding') from defensible, audit-ready product logic.|Recommended a conservative General Surgery pilot with mandatory HITL rather than claiming hospital-wide autonomous billing.|
