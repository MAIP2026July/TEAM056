**5.5 Comprehensive Evaluation Framework & Launch or Go-No-Go Thresholds**

|Metric Tier|Specific Evaluation Metric|Formula / Definition|Pilot Go / No-Go Launch Threshold|Why It Matters for PM Decision|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Technical (Completeness)|Deficiency Detection Recall (Sensitivity)|TP / (TP + FN) across all 5 mandatory NABH fields|>= 96.0% Recall (Must not miss real deficiencies)|A false negative passes an incomplete chart to NABH auditors or TPAs, risking accreditation citations.|
|Technical (Completeness)|Deficiency Precision|TP / (TP + FP) on flagged deficiencies|>= 85.0% Precision|Prevents alert fatigue among Deficiency Officers and physicians from false alarms.|
|Technical (ICD-10 Coding)|Top-3 In-Scope ICD-10 Recall & Macro F1|% of gold-standard surgical codes present in AI's top 3 suggestions|Top-3 Recall >= 88.0%; Top-1 Precision >= 80.0%|Ensures the correct code is immediately clickable on the right pane without manual dictionary hunting.|
|Technical (Calibration)|Expected Calibration Error (ECE)|Weighted gap between predicted confidence % and empirical accuracy|ECE <= 0.06 across confidence bins|Guarantees that an '85% High Confidence' badge genuinely corresponds to >=85% empirical accuracy.|
|Business & Operational|Average Chart Closure TAT & Coding Time Reduction|Mean hours from discharge to MRD sign-off vs. baseline|>= 25% TAT reduction (Target: >50% in pilot)|Proves tangible operational ROI and faster insurance claim submission velocity.|
|Business & Financial|TPA Coding/Documentation Denial Rate Delta|Pilot ward denial % vs. matched non-pilot surgical ward|>= 30% relative reduction in coding/doc denials|Directly validates revenue cycle protection for hospital leadership.|
|Trust & Safety Guardrail|Unchecked Miscoding Pass-Through Rate|% of pilot charts where coder accepted an incorrect AI code (found via 10% senior audit)|<= 2.0% error rate (Hard Blocker if > 3.0%)|Detects automation bias and protects the hospital from upcoding/undercoding penalties.|
