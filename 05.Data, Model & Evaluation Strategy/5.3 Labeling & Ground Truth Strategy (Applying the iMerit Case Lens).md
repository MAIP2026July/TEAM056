**5.3 Labeling & Ground Truth Strategy (Applying the iMerit Case Lens)** 

A major pitfall in healthcare AI is treating raw historical hospital billing logs as '100% ground truth'—when in reality historical logs contain the very coder inconsistencies we seek to fix. Drawing directly from the **iMerit medical data operations case lens** (iMerit Medical AI), we establish a **Dual-Coder Consensus Protocol** for our 200-chart Golden Evaluation Dataset: two senior certified coding officers independently code each validation chart; any disagreement (measured via Cohen's Kappa $\kappa$) is adjudicated by the MRD Department Head and a General Surgery attending clinician. During the live pilot, every human override in the UI automatically writes a structured JSON feedback tuple `(chart_hash, suggested_code, final_human_code, override_reason_id)` to build an expanding, hospital-specific benchmark dataset.
