**5.1 Translation of Business Problem into AI/ML Tasks**

Rather than treating 'medical records completion' as a vague monolithic prompt, we decompose the workflow into four distinct, auditable machine learning / NLP tasks:

**•	Task 1 — Structural & Semantic Completeness Classification (Multi-Label Binary Classification):** Given a parsed discharge summary document $D$, predict a 5-dimensional vector $Y_{comp} \in \{0, 1\}^5$ corresponding to the presence and clinical sufficiency of: (1) Signed Summary Header, (2) Final Diagnosis, (3) Procedure/Operative Note, (4) Discharge Condition, and (5) Follow-up Advice.

**•	Task 2 — Clinical Named Entity & Evidence Span Extraction (Sequence Labeling / Span Grounding):** Extract diagnostic entities, anatomical sites, laterality, severity, and negation modifiers (affirmed vs. ruled out vs. historical), returning exact character offsets $[s_i, e_i]$ in document $D$.

**•	Task 3 — Constrained ICD-10 Candidate Ranking (Retrieval-Augmented Multi-Label Ranking):** Given affirmed clinical spans, retrieve candidate codes from the curated General Surgery dictionary $\mathcal{C}_{50}$ ($|\mathcal{C}_{50}| = 50$) and output ranked probabilities $P(c_k | D)$ with calibrated confidence.

**•	Task 4 — Priority & Risk Triage Scoring (Deterministic + Probabilistic Rule Fusion):** Compute a composite priority score $S_{triage} = w_1(\text{ClaimValue}) + w_2(\text{AuditSensitivity}) + w_3(1 - \text{Confidence}) + w_4(\text{DeficiencySeverity})$ to sort the operational queue.
