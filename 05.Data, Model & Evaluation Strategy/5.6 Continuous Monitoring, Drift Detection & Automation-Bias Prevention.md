**5.6 Continuous Monitoring, Drift Detection & Automation-Bias Prevention**

Post-launch AI systems degrade silently if clinical templates change, new surgical procedures are introduced, or annual CMS/WHO ICD-10 updates take effect. More dangerously, as noted in Item 13 of our Topic Form, **human reviewers can develop automation complacency over time,** rubber-stamping AI suggestions without critical reading. We implement three active monitoring controls: (1) **Weekly Override & Dwell-Time Telemetry:** If a coder accepts >92% of suggestions or averages <4 seconds per chart, the MRD Head dashboard flags the user for supervisory coaching; (2) **10% Stratified Blind Audit Sampling:** 10% of all finalized charts are re-coded blind by a Senior Auditor; and (3) **Canary Quality Traps:** 2% of routine charts include a synthetic low-severity completeness or specificity check in training/audit mode to verify active human vigilance.
