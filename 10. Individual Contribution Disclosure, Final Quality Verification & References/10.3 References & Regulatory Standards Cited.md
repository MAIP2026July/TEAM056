**10.3 References & Regulatory Standards Cited**

•	National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers (NABH): Explore NABH Standards (6th Edition ACC-12 & ACC-13 Discharge Standards) and NABH Official Portal.

•	World Health Organization (WHO): International Statistical Classification of Diseases and Related Health Problems, 10th Revision (ICD-10 Official Browser).

•	Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India: The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 Official Gazette PDF and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

•	Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) & CMS: ICD-10-CM Classification of Diseases, Functioning, and Disability and FY 2025 ICD-10-CM Official Guidelines for Coding and Reporting.

•	National Health Authority (NHA), Government of India: Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) & National Health Claims Exchange (NHCX).

•	Clinical Documentation & Medical Record Audit Literature: Audit and Re-Audit of Discharge Summaries Completeness (PMC / National Library of Medicine) and Compliance of IPD Medical Records as per NABH Standards (IIHMR Delhi).

•	Medical Coding Denial & AI Annotation Case References: How to Identify and Prevent Medical Coding Denials (MediCodio AI), ICD-10 Medical Coding in Healthcare (eCare India), and Medical Data Annotation and Labeling for Healthcare AI (iMerit).
