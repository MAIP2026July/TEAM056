**4.1 Core UX Principle: A Review Copilot, Never an Autonomous Controller**

Because generative and predictive AI models are inherently probabilistic, the cardinal UX rule of Chart Ready AI is that the **interface must feel like an evidence-surfacing research assistant, never an overconfident autonomous controller.** In hospital revenue cycle and medical records management, a single silent hallucination—such as coding 'suspected malignancy' as a confirmed oncological diagnosis—can harm patient insurability, corrupt national health registries, and trigger payer fraud audits.
