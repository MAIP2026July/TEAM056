**4.6 Accessibility & Ergonomic Considerations for Hospital Staff**

MRD officers work 8-hour shifts reviewing dense clinical text on standard 1080p hospital desktop monitors. Chart Ready AI adheres to WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards: (1) all status badges pair color with distinct iconography (`✓`, `⚠`, `✗`) and text labels to support color-vision deficiency; (2) full keyboard shortcut navigation (`Alt+1/2/3` to inspect spans, `A` to Accept, `E` to Edit, `R` to Reject) reduces repetitive mouse strain; and (3) adjustable 125%–150% document zoom preserves layout alignment.
