**3.3 Product Requirements Document (PRD) Summary**

The table below defines the core functional and non-functional requirements for the Chart Ready AI Pilot MVP, explicitly incorporating data assumptions, probabilistic fallback logic, and risk guardrails:


|PRD Requirement ID & Module|Functional Specification & AI Role|Data Assumptions & Inputs|Probabilistic Fallback & Risk Guardrail|
|---|---|---|---|
|PRD-01: Document Ingestion & PHI De-Identification|Ingests scanned PDF or digital text discharge summaries from General Surgery; runs layout OCR and masks patient name, phone, address, and UHID into a reversible local session token.|Assumes >=85% of General Surgery discharge summaries are typed PDFs or legible scans (>=200 DPI) following standard hospital header templates.|If OCR word-confidence <75% (e.g., smudged handwritten addendum), halt AI coding and route directly to 'Manual Review — Poor Scan Quality' queue.|
|PRD-02: 5-Field NABH Completeness Classifier|Binary + multi-label classification across 5 mandatory elements: (1) Summary Present & Attending Signature, (2) Final Diagnosis, (3) Operative/Procedure Summary, (4) Condition at Discharge, (5) Follow-up & Medication Advice.|Requires access to NABH 6th Edition ACC-12/13 completeness rules and hospital physician signature registry metadata.|Any field with completeness confidence <80% defaults to 'Potential Deficiency — Human Verify' rather than silently passing as Complete.|
|PRD-03: Constrained ICD-10 Code Recommender|Semantic retrieval (RAG) + LLM ranking over the top 50 General Surgery ICD-10 codes; outputs code, official WHO title, confidence %, and verbatim evidence span.|Validated mapping of the department's top 50 historical ICD-10 codes covering >=80% of surgical volume, verified by Senior Coder.|If clinical diagnosis falls outside the top 50 codes (Out-of-Distribution), AI outputs 'Outside MVP Code Scope — Route to Standard Coder Lookup' rather than guessing.|
|PRD-04: Risk-Weighted Queue Triage Engine|Computes Priority Tier (High-Risk Track vs. Standard Queue) using Claim Type (TPA/Cashless vs. Cash), Claim Value (>₹1,00,000), Audit Category, and AI Confidence.|Requires basic billing header metadata (Payer Type, Estimated Bill Amount, Admission/Discharge timestamps).|If billing metadata is missing at ingestion, default chart to Medium Priority and alert Deficiency Officer to link admission episode.|
|PRD-05: Mandatory HITL Review & Telemetry Log|3-pane coder interface requiring explicit \[Accept\], \[Edit\], or \[Reject + Reason Code\] on every code before unlocking 'Export to Billing'.|Authenticated coder user IDs and structured dropdown taxonomy of 6 override reasons.|Hard UI lock prevents batch 'Accept All' on Low/Medium confidence codes; logs review dwell time (<5 sec triggers rubber-stamp warning).|
