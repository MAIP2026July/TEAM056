**3.4 Smallest Credible MVP Definition & Staged Roadmap with Proof Gates**

**Smallest Credible MVP Definition:** In alignment with Item 11 of our approved Topic Form, the MVP is strictly bounded to **one department (General Surgery)** and **one document type (Discharge Summary).** It delivers: (a) automated completeness verification against the 5 mandatory clinical fields, (b) automated ICD-10 diagnosis code suggestions restricted to the 30–50 most commonly used codes in General Surgery with confidence scores and text highlights, and (c) a queue dashboard displaying Complete vs. Deficient charts, priority track, and coder accept/edit/reject telemetry. It explicitly **excludes** hospital-wide rollout, live bi-directional HIS database writes, and full 70,000-code ICD-10 coverage.
