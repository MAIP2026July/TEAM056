**6.1 Monetization Model & Target Market Framing**

Chart Ready AI supports two complementary commercial deployment models depending on institutional context:
**• Model A — Internal Hospital Enterprise Value Case (Primary Pilot Framing):** Deployed internally by a hospital group (e.g., a 500-bed multi-specialty hospital processing 3,000 discharges/month, including 500 General Surgery discharges in the pilot). Value is captured directly through **coder labor productivity savings, working-capital acceleration from faster TPA claim filing, and avoided claim denials.**
**• Model B — B2B Healthcare SaaS Hybrid Subscription (External Commercialization):** Sold to mid-to-large NABH-accredited Indian hospitals (100 to 1,000+ beds) via a **Tiered Base Platform Subscription + Per-Chart Usage Fee:** ₹35,000/month base platform fee (covering up to 2,500 charts, VPC deployment, and NABH audit dashboards) plus ₹8.00 per additional discharge chart processed.
