**6.3 Scaling Constraints & Operational Implications**

**•	Latency & End-of-Morning Discharge Spikes:** Indian hospitals experience sharp discharge peaks between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM to free beds for afternoon admissions. Synchronous LLM calls can bottleneck during peak bursts. Operational Mitigation: Implement an asynchronous message queue (Redis/RabbitMQ) that pre-processes summaries in background workers within 20 seconds of draft save.

**•	Token Cost Growth with Multi-Page Complex Charts:** ICU stays exceeding 14 days generate 30–50 page case files. Sending entire raw charts to an LLM inflates token costs 10x. Operational Mitigation: Restrict ingestion strictly to the **Discharge Summary and Operative Note** (2–4 pages) rather than daily nursing vitals sheets, and enable prompt prefix caching for the static 50-code dictionary.

**•	Workflow Complexity & Legacy HIS Fragmentation:** Hospitals run heterogeneous Hospital Information Systems (vista, Karin, Practo Insta, custom Oracle forms). Operational Mitigation: Phase 1–2 operates via a zero-integration **Watched Folder / PDF Print-Stream Interceptor** and clipboard/CSV export, deferring deep HL7 FHIR integration to Phase 3 after value is proven.

**•	Governance Burden Across Specialties:** Scaling from General Surgery (50 codes) to Internal Medicine, Oncology, and Pediatrics requires specialty-specific completeness checklists and exclusion rules. Operational Mitigation: Appoint a 0.25 FTE **Clinical Coding Champion** per new department during onboarding to curate the specialty's top-100 code dictionary before turning on AI suggestions.
