**7.2 Governance Controls, Auditability & Hard Launch Blockers**

**HARD LAUNCH BLOCKERS (WHEN TO PAUSE OR REJECT LAUNCH)**
As AI Product Manager, Team 056 establishes four non-negotiable **Launch Blockers** that immediately halt pilot deployment or trigger an automatic rollback to manual FIFO processing:

**1. Privacy & Security Blocker:** Any failure of the local PHI de-identification pipeline to strip direct patient identifiers, or absence of a signed zero-data-retention VPC agreement compliant with the DPDP Act 2023.

**2. Clinical Completeness Safety Blocker:** If shadow validation shows Deficiency Recall < 95% (meaning >5% of genuinely incomplete charts are falsely marked 'Complete').

**3. Miscoding Pass-Through Blocker:** If weekly senior audit sampling reveals that >3.0% of finalized pilot charts contain an erroneous AI-suggested ICD-10 code accepted by a coder.

**4. Clinician Bypass Blocker:** Any software defect that allows a deficiency flag to be closed without authenticated physician sign-off.
