**2.4 AI Opportunity Matrix and RICE Feature Prioritization**

To avoid the common trap of attempting to 'automate the entire hospital EMR at once,' we evaluated seven candidate AI interventions using the **AI Opportunity Matrix** (Business Value vs. Technical/Operational Feasibility) and quantified priority via the **RICE Framework** (Reach x Impact x Confidence / Effort):

|Candidate AI Idea / Feature|Business Value|Feasibility|RICE Score (R×I×C/E)|Strategic Priority & Stage|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|F1. Automated 5-Field Completeness Classifier (Summary, Sign, Dx, Proc, Advice)|High (Stops NABH audit findings & TPA doc holds)|High (Clear rules + semantic check on 1 doc type)|95 × 3.0 × 0.90 / 0.9 = 285|P0 — Core MVP Wedge (Phase 1)|
|F2. Constrained ICD-10 Recommender (Top 30–50 General Surgery Codes + Confidence)|High (Cuts coding time 50%+; reduces miscoding)|High (RAG over curated 50-code set avoids long-tail errors)|80 × 3.0 × 0.80 / 0.8 = 240|P0 — Core MVP Wedge (Phase 1)|
|F3. Financial & Audit-Sensitivity Triage Queue Dashboard|High (Fast-tracks high-value TPA & high-risk charts)|High (Combines AI confidence + claim value metadata)|70 × 2.5 × 0.85 / 0.7 = 212|P0 — Core MVP Wedge (Phase 1)|
|F4. Automated Physician Deficiency Notification & Sign-off Tracker|Medium-High (Eliminates manual phone chasing)|Medium (Requires clinician mobile/WhatsApp/HIS routing)|65 × 2.0 × 0.80 / 1.0 = 104|P1 — Pilot Expansion (Phase 2)|
|F5. Full-Hospital ICD-10-CM & ICD-10-PCS Multi-Specialty Coding (70,000+ codes)|High (Hospital-wide billing coverage)|Low-Medium (High long-tail hallucination risk; needs specialty tuning)|100 × 3.0 × 0.55 / 3.0 = 55|P2 — Scale Stage (Phase 3)|
|F6. Bi-Directional EMR Integration & ABDM NHCX Claim Auto-Bundling|High (End-to-end straight-through processing)|Low (Legacy HIS vendor lock-in & HL7 FHIR maturity gaps)|100 × 2.5 × 0.50 / 3.5 = 36|P2 — Scale Stage (Phase 3)|
|F7. Autonomous Zero-Touch ICD-10 Billing Submission (No Human Coder)|Low / Negative (Extreme regulatory & denial hazard)|Very Low (Unacceptable error cost in clinical billing)|Rejected (Safety Gate)|Out of Scope — Permanently Blocked|
