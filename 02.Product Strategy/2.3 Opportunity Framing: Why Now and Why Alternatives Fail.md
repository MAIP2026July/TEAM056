**2.3 Opportunity Framing: Why Now and Why Alternatives Fail**

**Why This Problem Matters Now:** Indian private hospitals face dual margin compression from expanding cashless insurance schemes (including Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) and the National Health Claims Exchange — NHCX) and stricter NABH 6th Edition accreditation enforcement. According to healthcare revenue cycle benchmarks, coding inaccuracies and insufficient clinical documentation account for a substantial share of initial TPA claim rejections and queries (eCare India; OmniMD). Under NABH Discharge Process Guidelines, insurance/TPA discharges already take 4 to 6 hours at the bedside, followed by 48 to 72+ hours in MRD post-discharge chart completion and coding before final claim settlement.

**Why Current Non-AI Alternatives Are Weak:** Hospitals typically attempt three non-AI fixes: (1) Hiring more junior coders, which increases payroll costs and amplifies inter-coder inconsistency; (2) Enforcing rigid HIS form validation, which frustrates surgeons into typing placeholder text (e.g., 'NA' or 'As per chart') that passes software checks but fails NABH clinical audits; and (3) Rule-based keyword look-up tables, which fail whenever clinicians use narrative descriptions, regional medical abbreviations, or multi-clause differential diagnoses.

**Why AI Helps:** Modern instruction-tuned LLMs augmented with clinical retrieval can parse unstructured discharge narratives, distinguish confirmed diagnoses from ruled-out conditions, cross-check semantic completeness across five mandatory clinical dimensions, and output calibrated confidence probabilities alongside exact source-text citations.
