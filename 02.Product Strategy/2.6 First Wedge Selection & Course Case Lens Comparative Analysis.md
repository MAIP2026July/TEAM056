**2.6 First Wedge Selection & Course Case Lens Comparative Analysis**
First Wedge Definition: We restrict our initial deployment to **General Surgery Discharge Summaries** in one hospital unit, evaluating a fixed 5-point completeness checklist and recommending from the **top 30–50 most frequent surgical ICD-10 codes** (covering >80% of routine departmental volume such as appendicitis, cholelithiasis, inguinal hernia, diabetic foot ulcer, and bowel obstruction). This narrow wedge proves the core Extract → Check/Suggest → Human Confirms → Route decision loop without boiling the ocean.

**Integration of Course Case Lenses (Section 9 Requirement):** To ground our strategy in the MBA ZG583 curriculum, we benchmark Chart Ready AI against four core course cases:


|Course Case Lens|What Is Similar?|What Is Different?|What Lesson Transfers & What We Do Differently|
|---|---|---|---|
|iMerit (Data Operations & Labeling Quality)|Both rely on domain-specialized human reviewers (iMerit Medical AI) to structure noisy, real-world clinical narratives where generic crowd-labeling fails.|iMerit provides external annotation services for training datasets, whereas Chart Ready AI embeds real-time annotation capture into the hospital's live MRD coding workflow.|Lesson: Clinical AI is only as good as expert ground truth (iMerit Blog). We capture every coder Accept/Edit/Reject action as a structured ground-truth label to build a proprietary golden evaluation set.|

Our triage rules explicitly separate clinical completeness from patient socioeconomic status, and mandate periodic department-head override audits.|
