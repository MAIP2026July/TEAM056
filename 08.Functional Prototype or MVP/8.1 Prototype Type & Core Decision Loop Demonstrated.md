**8.1 Prototype Type & Core Decision Loop Demonstrated**

**Prototype Type:** In compliance with Section 7.8 of the Student Packet, we built and tested a **Self-Contained Interactive Browser Web Application Prototype (`chart_ready_ai_mvp.html`)** accompanied by full architectural wireframes (Figure 4). Rather than building a generic conversational chatbot—which the assignment rubric explicitly identifies as a weak prototype pattern—our functional MVP implements the exact **3-Pane MRD Triage, Completeness & ICD-10 Coding HITL Workbench** scoped to **General Surgery Discharge Summaries.**

**What Core Workflow & Decision Loop It Demonstrates:**

**1. Dynamic Risk-Prioritized Queue Triage:** Users can filter and switch between 4 realistic General Surgery discharge charts (spanning High-Risk TPA claims, Deficient unsigned charts, Low-Confidence complex comorbidities, and Routine clean surgical cases), plus ingest a custom discharge summary via the live **'Run AI Pre-Screen on New Chart'** simulator.

**2. 5-Field NABH Completeness Verification & Physician Routing:** Displays real-time pass/fail checks across the 5 mandatory clinical fields and enforces the **'Route Deficiency Alert to Physician'** workflow whenever a signature or follow-up plan is missing.

**3. Evidence-Grounded ICD-10 Recommendations (Top-50 Surgical Scope):** Renders candidate ICD-10 codes with confidence badges (High / Medium / Low) and interactive source-span highlighting in the clinical text.

**4. Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Lock & Override Telemetry:** The 'Finalize & Export to Billing' button remains strictly **LOCKED** until the human coder explicitly clicks **Accept, Edit** (modifying the code inline), or **Reject** (selecting a mandatory override reason from a governance modal). Every action dynamically updates the live **MRD Department Head Telemetry Dashboard** (tracking TAT, Acceptance Rate, Override Reasons, and Complacency Alerts).
