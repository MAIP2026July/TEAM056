**2. Product Strategy & Opportunity Framing: Focusing on Assisted Triage Rather Than Autonomous Coding**

**2.1 Product Vision**

**Product Vision (1–2 Sentence Statement):** 
To transform the Hospital Medical Records Department from a reactive, paper-chasing bottleneck into a high-velocity, audit-ready clinical intelligence hub by equipping every coding and completion officer with a transparent, human-governed AI copilot that eliminates preventable claim denials and documentation delays at discharge.
