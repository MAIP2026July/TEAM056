**2.2 Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) Framework Across MRD Personas**

Applying Clayton Christensen's Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) framework reveals that MRD staff do not want 'an AI chatbot'; they want reliable decision support under intense time pressure and regulatory scrutiny:

|Target Persona|Situation (When I am...)|Motivation (I want to...)|Expected Outcome (So that I can...)|
|---|---|---|---|
|MRD Coding Officer (Primary User)|Reviewing a batch of 25–40 post-discharge surgical case sheets with dense operative notes and TPA billing deadlines|Instantly see highlighted clinical evidence paired with the top probable ICD-10 diagnosis and procedure codes and confidence scores|Assign accurate, defensible codes in under 8 minutes per chart without missing secondary comorbidities or triggering TPA claim denials|
|MRD Deficiency Officer (Secondary User)|Auditing newly arrived discharge files before clinicians leave their shift or rotate off ward duty|Automatically detect missing signatures, absent discharge conditions, or incomplete follow-up instructions upon file ingestion|Route targeted, single-click deficiency alerts to the responsible physician immediately rather than manually flipping through paper charts|
|MRD Department Head (Beneficiary)|Preparing for quarterly NABH surveillance audits and reviewing monthly TPA rejection reports with hospital finance|Prioritize high-value and audit-sensitive charts first while tracking coder override rates and department-wide TAT bottlenecks|Maintain >=98% NABH documentation compliance, accelerate insurance cash recovery, and prevent systematic coding errors|
